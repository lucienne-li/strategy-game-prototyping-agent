For beginning game-design and decision-science students. Predict an outcome, play a short card activity, and explain how a limited energy budget affects what you can achieve.

✦
How to use this activity

1. Start the guided activity. 2. Predict the enemy HP after three strikes. 3. Play, check your prediction, and restart to try again.

Guided practice builds a fixed, playable example without AI generation. AI customization, when configured, is limited to the same card-combat rules.

Agent result

Learning goal

Calculate the maximum damage from a limited energy budget and explain why an action can be valid yet insufficient to reach a goal.

This is a fixed rules exercise, not an assessment of your general decision-making ability. Written reflections are not automatically graded.
